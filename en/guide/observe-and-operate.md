# Observe and operate

Use traces to follow a request, metrics to measure behavior over time and logs
to retain events worth investigating. Configure the exporter and backend as
well as the application instrumentation.

Health and readiness tell an orchestrator whether to send traffic. Reflection
lets tools inspect the contract of a server at a known address; it is not a
registry that discovers other running services. Choose deliberately which
operational endpoints your authorization policy exposes.
